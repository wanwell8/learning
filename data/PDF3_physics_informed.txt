The well- trained GGAT layer of the surrogate model is then employed as the representation network and integrated with the deep auto-encoder (DAE\ to extract informative features for the decision-making of the soft actor critic (SAC\ method, the parameters of which are optimized based on the reward value calculated by the surrogate model. The systematical integration of the physics-informed representation network, SAC agent, and the surrogate model yields the following contributions: The proposed voltage control method is robust to anoma- lous measurements. This enables the SAC method to make control deci- sions robust to anomalous measurements, a signiÞcant advantage over existing DRL algorithms. This is achieved by the synergistic integration of the DRL agent and the sur- rogate model, which captures the relationship between the measurements and voltage magnitude and provides a reward signal during the training of the control agent. This allows it to reduce the reliance on accurate line parameters that are difÞcult to obtain in distribution system. In addition, it only needs partial observations of distribution systems.
